Three-Day Coastal Weekend Plan

Friday afternoon is reserved for the drive to Seabrook, a fictional harbor
town about two hours from home. Check into the small guesthouse after four,
then walk to the public pier before sunset. Dinner is planned at a casual
seafood cafe near the water. If the weather turns windy, use the covered
market hall instead of the pier.

Saturday morning starts with breakfast and a visit to the lighthouse trail.
The route is roughly four miles round trip and includes a steep section, so
pack water, a light rain jacket, and comfortable shoes. The tide pool boardwalk
is best visited near low tide; check the posted tide chart at the trailhead
and stay behind the marked ropes. In the afternoon, browse the local craft
market and take a break at the botanical garden.

For dinner, reserve a table at a family restaurant that serves vegetarian
options as well as grilled fish. Keep the evening flexible in case the town
holds a harbor concert. On Sunday, visit the small maritime museum when it
opens at ten. The exhibits cover boat building, fog signals, and the history
of the old ferry route. Leave by early afternoon to avoid the busiest road
traffic.

The packing list includes a reusable bottle, sunscreen, binoculars, phone
charger, printed reservation details, and a small first-aid pouch. Bring a
card and a modest amount of cash for market stalls. The plan avoids tight
connections and leaves time for weather changes, rest, and unplanned stops.
