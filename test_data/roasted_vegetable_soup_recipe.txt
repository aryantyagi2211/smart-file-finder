Roasted Tomato and White Bean Soup

This weeknight soup combines roasted tomatoes, white beans, and herbs for a
filling meal that keeps well for lunch. Heat the oven to 220 degrees Celsius.
Cut one kilogram of ripe tomatoes into halves and place them on a tray with
one chopped red onion and four garlic cloves. Add two tablespoons of olive
oil, a pinch of salt, black pepper, and dried oregano. Roast for about thirty
minutes, until the tomato edges are browned and the onion is soft.

Warm a tablespoon of olive oil in a heavy pot. Add one diced carrot and one
celery stalk, cooking gently for five minutes. Stir in a teaspoon of smoked
paprika and a small pinch of chili flakes. Pour in the roasted vegetables
and their juices, followed by four cups of vegetable stock. Simmer for ten
minutes so the flavors combine.

Rinse and drain two cans of cannellini beans. Add them to the pot with a
handful of chopped basil. Use an immersion blender to puree about half the
soup, leaving enough whole beans for a hearty texture. If the soup is too
thick, add a little stock or water. Taste and adjust the salt and pepper.

Serve hot with toasted bread and a squeeze of lemon. A spoonful of yogurt
or grated cheese can be added at the table. For a dairy-free version, skip
the toppings. Cool leftovers promptly and refrigerate in a sealed container
for up to four days, or freeze individual portions for an easy future lunch.
