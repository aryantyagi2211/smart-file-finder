Community Repair Cafe Planning Notes

The volunteer team met on Tuesday evening to plan the spring repair cafe at
the neighborhood library. The event will run from ten in the morning until
two in the afternoon, with setup beginning at nine. Visitors can bring small
household items such as lamps, fans, bicycles, clothing, and kitchen tools.
The team will not accept large appliances, devices with swollen batteries,
or anything that cannot be safely unplugged.

Mira will reserve the community room and confirm the tables with the library.
Owen will bring the bicycle stand, tire levers, and a floor pump. Priya will
organize the sewing station and prepare spare buttons, needles, thread, and
patches. The electronics table needs two power strips, a multimeter, and
clear signs asking visitors to remove batteries before inspection.

Each visitor should receive a numbered card so volunteers can track the
queue without collecting personal details. A greeter will explain that
repairs are collaborative and not guaranteed. If a repair needs a part, the
volunteer will write down the part name and suggest local suppliers. The
team will bring a cash box for optional donations, but there is no entry fee.

Before closing, volunteers will sort usable leftover materials into labeled
boxes and recycle damaged cables. A short feedback card will ask which
station was helpful and what should change next time. The team agreed to
review attendance, safety notes, and supply costs at the following meeting.
